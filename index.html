AI & automation

Where AI actually sits in the funnel.

Not a slide of logos — this is the stack running inside the marketing operation today, and what changed once each piece went live.

ToolSits whereWhat it changed
Retell AI Call centre, outbound Automated calling and lead scoring, so agents spend time on qualified conversations instead of dialling
Zoho CRM + SQL Funnel & targeting Zero lead leakage across four regions, with SQL pulls feeding campaign targeting directly
WhatsApp Business API Nurture Always-on, AI-assisted sequences that keep prospective learners engaged without a human on every message
HeyGen + ElevenLabs Video & localisation Localised video at a speed a production team couldn't match — part of what supported the Southeast Asia push
Power BI Reporting Live CAC, ROAS and conversion dashboards, replacing after-the-fact spreadsheet reporting
Zapier + Canva Content ops Content pipeline running between CRM, creative and channels with far less manual handoff

The test I use for any AI tool: does it remove a manual step without removing a human decision. Retell AI scores leads; a person still decides who to call back. HeyGen produces the video; the script and compliance language are still mine.
